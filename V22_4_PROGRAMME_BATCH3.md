# V22.4 — Programme Database Batch 3

This batch expands the verified catalogue into health sciences, built environment, psychology, communication and creative media.

## Records added

- UTM: 9 built-environment programmes
- Taylor's: 9 health, social-science and built-environment programmes
- UCSI: 8 health, psychology and built-environment programmes
- Sunway: 9 health, psychology, communication and design programmes
- MMU: 6 communication and creative-media programmes
- **Total: 41 programmes**

## Public fee policy

Exact tuition values remain hidden from the public site. Advisors may maintain internal fee records separately, but public pages should direct users to request the latest official tuition quote.

## Verification

Every row contains an official-source URL and a verification date. If a duration or intake was not clearly available in the official source used for this batch, it was intentionally left blank rather than guessed.

## Run

Execute `supabase/migrations/005_programme_batch3_health_built_media.sql` in the Supabase SQL Editor after migrations 001–004.
