# V23 — Programme Comparison

This release adds an objective programme comparison flow to YAZ Education.

## User flow

1. Open the programme directory.
2. Select **Add to compare** on two or three verified programme cards.
3. A fixed comparison tray appears and persists in the browser.
4. Select **Compare now**.
5. Review the programmes side by side.
6. Contact a YAZ advisor with the compared programmes pre-filled in WhatsApp.

## Comparison attributes

- Programme name
- University
- Field
- Study level
- Campus/location
- Duration
- Study mode
- Intakes
- Academic requirements
- English requirements
- Accreditation/recognition
- Tuition policy (advisor contact only; no public static fee)
- Last verified date
- Official source

## Rules

- Maximum three programmes at once.
- At least two programmes are required for a useful comparison.
- No university/programme is ranked or declared a winner.
- Unknown values display as not yet verified rather than being invented.
- Exact tuition fees remain private and are confirmed by a YAZ advisor.
- Comparison combinations are marked noindex to avoid indexing large numbers of query-based duplicate pages.
